Welcome to AVM

A ministry guided by purpose and rooted in faith.

Reaching the lost. Teaching the Word. Developing disciples. Restoring lives.

At Anointed Vision Ministry, we are committed to helping people encounter Jesus Christ, grow in His Word, discover their God-given purpose, and become equipped for Kingdom service.
