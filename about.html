Our Mission & Vision

Ministering hope and wholeness through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission

Called to reach, teach, and restore.

The mission of Anointed Vision Ministry (AVM) is to minister hope and wholeness through the Gospel of Jesus Christ by winning souls, making disciples, teaching the uncompromising Word of God, and equipping believers to discover their God-given purpose and live holy lives.

We are committed to creating a Christ-centered, Spirit-led, and loving environment where individuals and families can experience spiritual growth, healing, restoration, deliverance, and transformation through the power of the Holy Spirit and the truth of God’s Word.

Through Discipleship, Soul-Winning, and our commitment to being a Teaching Church, we seek to develop spiritually mature believers who are equipped to serve, lead, evangelize, and fulfill the Great Commission.

Guided by our A.N.O.I.N.T. mandate, we will empower believers through Christ, never neglect evangelism, reach beyond the walls of the church through outreach, cultivate impeccable fellowship, practice faithful stewardship, and teach proper worship.

Through Apostolic Leadership and Kingdom Building, Anointed Vision Ministry strives to strengthen families, serve our community, develop leaders, restore broken lives, and advance the Kingdom of God by demonstrating the love of Christ in both word and deed.

Our mission is to help people encounter Jesus Christ, grow in the knowledge of His Word, walk in holiness, discover their purpose, and become productive members of the Body of Christ.

At Anointed Vision Ministry, we are committed to reaching the lost, teaching the Word, developing disciples, restoring lives, and advancing God’s Kingdom—one soul at a time.

It’s Because of the Vision, We Will Not Perish.

Our Vision

Faithful to the vision.

The vision of Anointed Vision Ministry (AVM) is to be a Christ-centered, Spirit-led, and purpose-driven ministry committed to Ministering Hope and Wholeness through the preaching and teaching of the Gospel of Jesus Christ, the making of disciples, the winning of souls, and the development of believers into mature, empowered, and purpose-filled followers of Christ.

We envision a church where lives are transformed by the power of God’s Word, families are strengthened, spiritual gifts are cultivated, leaders are equipped, and the love of Christ is demonstrated through authentic fellowship, compassionate outreach, and faithful stewardship.

Through Apostolic Leadership and Kingdom Building, AVM is committed to creating an atmosphere where people can discover their God-given purpose, experience spiritual growth, walk in holiness, and become effective witnesses for Jesus Christ in their homes, communities, and throughout the world.

Guided by the principles of our A.N.O.I.N.T. mandate, we will empower believers, evangelize the lost, serve our community, cultivate impeccable fellowship, practice faithful stewardship, and teach proper worship.

Our vision is to build a spiritually grounded, biblically sound, and mission-focused ministry that reaches the lost, restores the broken, develops disciples, equips leaders, and advances the Kingdom of God—one soul, one family, and one community at a time.

At Anointed Vision Ministry, we believe that when God gives the vision, He also provides the grace, wisdom, and strength to fulfill it. We will remain faithful to the assignment, committed to the Word, and surrendered to the will of God, knowing that it is because of the vision, we will not perish.
