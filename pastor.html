Meet our pastor

Rev. Dr. Morris M. Sanders Jr.

Founder & Senior Pastor of Anointed Vision Ministry (AVM) · Selma, North Carolina

“It’s Because of the Vision, We Will Not Perish.” — Proverbs 29:18 (KJV)

Our leadership

A life of faith and service

Rev. Dr. Morris M. Sanders Jr. is the Founder and Senior Pastor of Anointed Vision Ministry in Selma, North Carolina. He serves alongside his wife, Lady Evelyn M. Sanders, affectionately known as Lady E.

The biography below shares his journey from military service to pastoral leadership and the founding vision of AVM.

Early Life and Background

Rev. Dr. Morris M. Sanders Jr. was born on September 30, 1965, in Brooklyn, New York, and raised in Smithfield, North Carolina. He graduated from Smithfield-Selma Senior High School in 1984 and attended Elizabeth City State University for one semester before beginning his military service.

Military Service

In October 1986, Rev. Dr. Sanders enlisted in the United States Army, serving until 1992. During his military career, he served in Germany, Korea, and Fort Bragg, North Carolina, and participated in Operation Desert Storm. He was honorably discharged.

His military experience helped shape his discipline, perseverance, and commitment to leadership and service. The values he developed while serving his country continue to influence his approach to ministry and his commitment to serving God’s people.

The Call to Ministry

Rev. Dr. Sanders answered God’s call to preach while a member of Saint Mark African Methodist Episcopal Church in Smithfield, North Carolina, under the pastoral leadership of the late Rev. Dr. R. L. Holmes.

He subsequently served as pastor of Rue Chapel in New Bern, Saint Paul in Teachey, Lee’s Chapel in Smithfield, and Lebanon in Littleton, North Carolina, within the Northern District of the African Methodist Episcopal Church.

During thirteen years of pastoral service in the AME Church, he developed experience in preaching, biblical teaching, pastoral care, church leadership, and spiritual formation. These years provided an important foundation for the ministry assignment that would follow.

The Vision Behind Anointed Vision Ministry

Following what he understood to be God’s divine direction, Rev. Dr. Sanders stepped forward in faith to establish Anointed Vision Ministry in Selma, North Carolina. As Founder and Senior Pastor, he leads AVM with a commitment to winning souls, developing disciples, teaching the Word of God, and helping people discover their God-given purpose.

AVM is the expression of the vision he has committed his life to serving. The ministry seeks to reach people with the Gospel, encourage spiritual maturity, strengthen families, develop leaders, and equip believers to live holy lives and participate meaningfully in God’s Kingdom.

The ministry’s declaration is: “It’s Because of the Vision, We Will Not Perish.”

Inspired by Proverbs 29:18, this motto reflects the importance of pursuing God’s direction with faith, prayer, obedience, and perseverance.

The A.N.O.I.N.T. Ministry Philosophy

The mission and ministry philosophy of AVM are expressed through the acronym A.N.O.I.N.T.:

  • A — Always Empower: Empowering believers through faith and the Word of God (Philippians 4:13).
  • N — Never Neglect Evangelism: Remaining committed to sharing the Gospel (Romans 10:14).
  • O — Outreach: Reaching beyond the church walls to serve others (Mark 16:15).
  • I — Impeccable Fellowship: Promoting Christian unity and fellowship (2 Corinthians 13:14).
  • N — Never Neglect Proper Stewardship: Faithfully managing God’s gifts and resources (1 Peter 4:10).
  • T — Teach Proper Worship: Teaching believers to honor and worship God (1 Chronicles 16:23–31).

Through these principles, Rev. Dr. Sanders seeks to cultivate a ministry centered on discipleship, soul-winning, biblical instruction, fellowship, worship, and Kingdom building.

Theological Education

Rev. Dr. Sanders has pursued theological education to strengthen his biblical knowledge and ministry leadership. His academic achievements include:

  • Associate Degree in Biblical Studies — North Carolina College of Theology
  • Bachelor’s Degree in Theology — GAP Ministries College of Theology
  • Master’s I Degree in Theology — GAP Ministries College of Theology
  • Master’s II Degree in Theology — GAP Ministries College of Theology
  • Doctoral Degree in Theology — GAP Ministries College of Theology

His educational background complements his pastoral experience and commitment to proclaiming the Gospel and teaching biblical principles.

Fraternal Affiliation

Rev. Dr. Sanders is a proud member of Kappa Lambda Chi Military Fraternity, Incorporated. His fraternal affiliation reflects his continued connection to military brotherhood, fellowship, leadership, and service.

As a veteran, fraternity brother, and pastor, he values commitment, unity, accountability, and service to others.

Family and Partnership in Ministry

Rev. Dr. Sanders is a devoted husband to Lady Evelyn M. Sanders, affectionately known as Lady E. Together, they share a commitment to serving God and supporting the vision of Anointed Vision Ministry.

He is also a proud father of four children: Morris, Deven, Seoulita, and Tre’seon. His family includes six grandchildren and three great-grandchildren. His family is a source of joy and gratitude and an important part of the legacy he seeks to build through faith, love, perseverance, and service.

Together, Rev. Dr. Sanders and Lady E continue to encourage others, strengthen families, and serve God’s people through their partnership in life and ministry.

His Continuing Commitment

Rev. Dr. Sanders remains committed to preaching the Gospel, making disciples, developing leaders, reaching the lost, and helping people discover and fulfill their God-given purpose.

His journey—from serving his country in the United States Army to serving God’s people through pastoral ministry—reflects a continuing commitment to faith, perseverance, and service.

As Founder and Senior Pastor of Anointed Vision Ministry, he continues to build upon the foundation of his thirteen years of AME pastoral service while advancing the vision he believes God has entrusted to him.

His desire is to see souls saved, lives transformed, families strengthened, believers discipled, and leaders equipped for the work of God’s Kingdom.

His vision is rooted in God. His commitment is to the people. His mission is the Gospel. His ministry is dedicated to building the Kingdom.
